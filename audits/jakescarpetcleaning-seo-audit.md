# SEO Audit: Jake's Certified Air Duct & Carpet Cleaning
**Website:** jakescarpetcleaning.com
**Audit Date:** March 31, 2026
**Business Type:** Local Service — Carpet & Air Duct Cleaning
**Location:** Littleton, CO (Denver Metro)

---

## Executive Summary

Jake's Certified Air Duct & Carpet Cleaning has strong brand differentiation (201% guarantee, IICRC/NADCA certified, botanical products) and a perfect 5-star Google rating — but the website is significantly underperforming in organic search. The core problems are a near-invisible index footprint (only 3–4 pages indexed), missing location signals in title tags, and absent service-specific landing pages that would capture high-intent local search traffic.

**Overall SEO Health: 4/10 — Needs significant work**

### Top 3 Priority Issues
1. **No location in homepage title tag** — Google can't associate this site with "Littleton CO" or "Denver" carpet cleaning searches
2. **Only 3–4 pages indexed** — The site has almost no organic surface area to rank for anything
3. **Missing service + location landing pages** — No pages targeting searches like "air duct cleaning Littleton CO" or "carpet cleaning Denver"

### Quick Wins
- Fix the title tag on the homepage (30 min)
- Add city/state to meta descriptions (1 hr)
- Submit XML sitemap to Google Search Console (15 min)

## Technical SEO Findings

### 1. Extremely Low Index Count
- **Issue:** Only 3–4 pages appear in Google's index (homepage, /mission-vision-values, /meet-the-team, /tile-grout-cleaning-services/)
- **Impact:** HIGH — A site with 3 indexed pages cannot compete for meaningful keyword volume
- **Evidence:** `site:jakescarpetcleaning.com` returns 3 results
- **Fix:** Audit robots.txt and meta robots tags. Check Search Console Coverage report for "Excluded" URLs. Ensure all service pages have `index, follow` directives and are reachable from internal links.
- **Priority:** Critical

### 2. Missing XML Sitemap (Unconfirmed)
- **Issue:** No sitemap reference visible from indexed pages; likely not submitted to Search Console
- **Impact:** HIGH — Google may not be discovering new/updated pages
- **Fix:** Verify sitemap exists at `/sitemap.xml`. If using Squarespace/WordPress/Wix, enable the built-in sitemap. Submit to Google Search Console.
- **Priority:** Critical

### 3. Robots.txt — Unverified
- **Issue:** Unable to confirm robots.txt contents; if pages are being blocked, this would explain the low index count
- **Fix:** Check `jakescarpetcleaning.com/robots.txt` — ensure no `Disallow: /` or accidental blocks on service pages
- **Priority:** High

### 4. HTTPS — Likely OK
- **Issue:** Domain should be serving HTTPS; verify no mixed content warnings
- **Impact:** Low if already resolved
- **Fix:** Confirm `https://` redirects properly and SSL certificate is valid
- **Priority:** Low

## On-Page SEO Findings

### 5. Homepage Title Tag Missing Location (CRITICAL)
- **Issue:** Current title is `"Home - Jake's Certified Air Duct & Carpet Cleaning"` — no city, no state, no geo-modifier
- **Impact:** HIGH — Local service businesses live and die by geo-targeted searches. "carpet cleaning Littleton CO" and "air duct cleaning Denver" will not associate with this page.
- **Fix:** Change to: `"Carpet & Air Duct Cleaning in Littleton, CO | Jake's Certified"`
- **Priority:** Critical

### 6. "Home -" Title Prefix
- **Issue:** Leading with "Home -" wastes the most SEO-valuable real estate in the title tag
- **Impact:** Medium — Reduces click-through and keyword relevance signal
- **Fix:** Remove "Home -" entirely. Lead with the primary keyword + location.
- **Priority:** High

### 7. Homepage Meta Description — Location Likely Missing
- **Issue:** Based on the title pattern, meta description likely doesn't include "Littleton CO," "Denver," or a strong local CTA
- **Impact:** Medium — Meta descriptions don't directly affect rankings but drive click-through rate from SERPs
- **Fix:** Write: `"IICRC & NADCA certified carpet and air duct cleaning in Littleton, CO. Botanical products, 201% guarantee. Serving the Denver metro. Call (303) 309-9797."`
- **Priority:** High

### 8. Service Pages Not Indexed or Non-Existent
- **Issue:** No evidence of indexed pages for: carpet cleaning, air duct cleaning, area rug cleaning, furniture cleaning, tile & grout (only partially), or water/fire restoration
- **Impact:** HIGH — These are the exact pages that rank for commercial-intent local searches
- **Fix:** Create or fix individual service pages:
  - `/carpet-cleaning-littleton-co/`
  - `/air-duct-cleaning-littleton-co/`
  - `/area-rug-cleaning-denver/`
  - `/upholstery-cleaning-littleton-co/`
  - `/water-damage-restoration-denver/`
- **Priority:** Critical

### 9. No Location/Service-Area Pages
- **Issue:** No evidence of pages targeting surrounding Denver metro cities (Denver, Englewood, Highlands Ranch, Parker, Centennial, Aurora)
- **Impact:** High — Competitors with location pages will rank for these city-specific searches
- **Fix:** Create service area pages for the top 5–8 cities served. Minimum: unique intro paragraph, embedded Google Map, NAP info, service list, review snippet.
- **Priority:** High

### 10. Meet the Team Page — Good E-E-A-T Signal (Keep)
- **Issue:** Page exists at `/meet-the-team` — this is actually a positive E-E-A-T signal
- **Impact:** Positive — Shows real humans behind the business
- **Fix:** Ensure team bios mention certifications (IICRC, NADCA) and years of experience. Add photos with descriptive alt text.
- **Priority:** Medium (enhancement)

## Content Quality Assessment

### 11. Thin Content Risk
- **Issue:** With only 3–4 indexed pages, most pages are likely thin (under 400 words of unique content)
- **Impact:** HIGH — Google's Helpful Content guidelines penalize thin, low-value pages
- **Fix:** Each service page needs at minimum: 600+ words covering the process, benefits, certifications, before/after expectations, FAQs, and local references
- **Priority:** High

### 12. Blog / Content Strategy — Not Present
- **Issue:** No evidence of a blog or educational content hub
- **Impact:** Medium — Missing opportunity to rank for informational queries ("how often should I clean air ducts," "carpet cleaning vs dry cleaning," etc.) that build trust and funnel leads
- **Fix:** Add 1 blog post per month targeting FAQ-style local service questions
- **Priority:** Medium (long-term)

### 13. Strong Brand Differentiators Not Leveraged for SEO
- **Issue:** The 201% guarantee, IICRC/NADCA certifications, and botanical products are compelling but may not be in the right SEO-optimized places (title, H1, service page headers)
- **Impact:** Medium — These are conversion drivers AND trust signals that Google rewards
- **Fix:** Mention certifications and guarantee in page titles and meta descriptions where natural. Create a dedicated "Why Jake's" or "Our Process" page.
- **Priority:** Medium

## Local SEO Findings

### 14. Google Business Profile (GBP) — Verify Optimization
- **Issue:** Cannot confirm GBP optimization status from website audit alone
- **Impact:** HIGH — For local service businesses, GBP is often the #1 driver of leads
- **Fix:**
  - Confirm business name, address, phone match exactly: Jake's Certified Air Duct & Carpet Cleaning | 12661 W Brandt Dr, Littleton, CO 80127 | (303) 309-9797
  - Add all services to GBP
  - Upload photos (before/after, team, equipment)
  - Post weekly updates
  - Respond to all reviews
- **Priority:** Critical

### 15. NAP Consistency
- **Issue:** Business name on website ("Jake's Certified Air Duct & Carpet Cleaning") must match exactly across Yelp, BBB, Google, Facebook, and all directories
- **Impact:** High — Inconsistent NAP signals confuse Google's local algorithm
- **Fix:** Audit all citations. The Yelp listing shows address: 12661 W Brandt Dr, Littleton, CO 80127. Ensure exact match everywhere.
- **Priority:** High

### 16. Schema Markup — Likely Missing
- **Issue:** No JSON-LD schema markup visible; JS-rendered schema cannot be verified via static audit
- **Impact:** Medium — LocalBusiness schema helps Google understand business type, location, hours, and contact info
- **Fix:** Add `LocalBusiness` (or `CarpetCleaner`/`HomeAndConstructionBusiness`) schema with:
  - `name`, `address`, `telephone`, `openingHours`
  - `priceRange`, `areaServed`
  - `aggregateRating` (tie to Google reviews)
- **Priority:** High

### 17. Reviews — 60 Yelp Reviews, 5-Star Google Rating
- **Issue:** Strong review signal but reviews may not be embedded on the website
- **Impact:** Medium — On-site review snippets and schema improve CTR and trust
- **Fix:** Add review schema markup and embed 3–5 recent 5-star reviews on homepage and service pages
- **Priority:** Medium
