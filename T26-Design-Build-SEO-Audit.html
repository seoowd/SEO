Overall Health

1Executive Summary

The site has a healthy technical foundation — every page returns a clean 200 OK, all pages are indexable, and canonical tags are correctly configured. There are no broken pages, redirect chains, or indexing blocks.

However, the audit surfaced several on-page and content gaps that are likely holding back rankings — especially on the high-value service pages (kitchen, bathroom, home, and landscape remodeling). The two most impactful issues are:

  • Missing H1 headings on 6 of the most important pages, including the homepage.
  • A leftover WordPress placeholder description on the homepage ("Just another construction site"), wasting your most valuable search snippet.

Site speed is also a concern, with several pages showing very slow server response times. The good news: the highest-impact fixes are quick and inexpensive.

2Priority Issues — Fix First

HighHomepage description is a placeholder

The homepage meta description currently reads "Just another construction site" — the default WordPress tagline. It appears directly in Google results and undermines credibility and click-through on your most visited page.

Fix: Replace with a compelling, keyword-rich description, e.g. "T26 Design Build Co. delivers expert home, kitchen, bathroom & landscape remodeling in Phoenix, Scottsdale & Chandler. Book your free estimate today."

HighMissing H1 headings on 6 key pages

These pages have no H1 tag, weakening their relevance for target keywords. They currently lead with an H2 instead.

HighHomepage title is too short & under-optimized

The title is just "T26 Design Build" (16 characters) — it uses almost none of the available space and contains no service or location keywords.

Fix: "Phoenix Home Remodeling & Design Build Contractor | T26" (~50–55 characters).

Fix: Trim each to a single, tight sentence with a call to action. The bathroom & home-remodeling pages are already a good length — use them as the template.

MediumThin content on portfolio pages

The six project pages (Grand Vista Duplex, Cedar Cabin, Chandler Home Remodel, etc.) contain only 52–68 words each — essentially image galleries with little text for Google to rank.

Fix: Add 150–250 words per project covering scope of work, location, materials, and outcome. This also strengthens local relevance ("Chandler", "Scottsdale", etc.).

MediumSlow server response times

4Low-Priority & Housekeeping

  • Typo  The "Our Work" page reads "home and ourdoor remodeling projects" — should be "outdoor". Worth a full-site proofread.
  • Internal links  The Scottsdale Kitchen Remodel page has only 1 internal link. Link portfolio pages from related service pages to spread authority.
  • Readability  Several service pages score "Fairly Hard". Shorten sentences and break up paragraphs.
  • HTTP/2  Site is served over HTTP/1.1. Upgrading to HTTP/2 (a host/CDN setting) gives a small speed gain.

5What's Working Well

  • All 15 pages return 200 OK and are indexable — no crawl or indexation errors.
  • Canonical tags are present and self-referencing on every page.
  • Clean, keyword-friendly URLs (e.g. /kitchen-remodel-phoenix/).
  • Logical architecture — crawl depth never exceeds 2 clicks.
  • Most service-page titles are well-formed and include location keywords.
